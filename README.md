## 📖 Overview

Lawyers Case Diary is a web-based legal case management platform built to streamline the day-to-day operations of lawyers and legal practitioners. The system provides a centralized workspace for managing cases, tracking court dates, maintaining client records, and organizing legal documents.

The platform also incorporates AI-powered features such as case summarization and semantic search, enabling lawyers to quickly retrieve relevant information and improve productivity.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👨‍⚖️ Case Management

* Create and manage legal cases
* Track case status and progress
* Maintain complete case history
* Categorize cases by type and priority

### 👥 Client Management

* Store client information securely
* Manage client profiles and contact details
* Associate multiple cases with clients

### 📅 Court Hearing Management

* Schedule hearings and court dates
* Receive reminders for upcoming hearings
* Track hearing outcomes and updates

### 📄 Document Management

* Upload and organize legal documents
* Maintain case-specific records
* Easy access to important files

### 🔍 Smart Search

* Advanced search across cases and records
* Semantic search for faster information retrieval
* Filter cases by status, client, date, or category

### 🤖 AI-Powered Features

* AI-assisted case summarization
* Quick overview of lengthy case histories
* Improved legal workflow efficiency

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics

* Case statistics and summaries
* Upcoming hearings overview
* Recent activity tracking
* Productivity insights

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Managing legal cases manually often leads to scattered information, missed court dates, and inefficient workflows. Lawyers require a centralized platform where they can efficiently manage clients, cases, documents, and legal proceedings.

Lawyers Case Diary addresses these challenges by providing a structured and user-friendly digital solution tailored specifically for legal professionals.

---

## 💡 Solution

The platform digitizes the complete legal case management process by:

* Centralizing case information
* Automating legal workflow tracking
* Improving case retrieval through AI-powered search
* Enhancing productivity through smart summaries
* Providing real-time visibility into case progress
